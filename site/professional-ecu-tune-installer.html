What makes an ECU tune install "professional"?

Anyone can email you a file. A professional install means a licensed tuner, an in-person installation, and verification on your actual vehicle:

  • Licensed VFTuner PRO Tuners — every calibration is built by a licensed tuner using the full Toyota/Lexus toolset, not a generic off-the-shelf file.
  • Installed in person — you meet your installer at an event near you, watch the install, and get your questions answered face-to-face.
  • Emissions verified — factory emissions systems stay fully intact and every calibration is verified with a 5-gas analyzer during drive-cycle testing. Check-engine lights are never disabled.
  • Dialed in, not dropped off — custom calibrations are refined over multiple log reviews for the best result on your specific build.
