What "back to stock" means here

  • Byte-for-byte factory stock — the ECU runs exactly the factory calibration file again, not an "almost stock" approximation.
  • About 20 minutes — a return-to-stock flash is a normal appointment, not a project.
  • Anytime, for any reason — resale, inspection, dealer visit, or just changing your mind.
  • Reversible in both directions — returning to stock doesn't burn anything; your calibration can come back when you want it.

Why reversibility matters

It converts the tune from a permanent decision into a choice you keep making. Concretely:

  • Resale — some buyers pay more for a documented tuned truck; others want stock. Reversibility lets you decide at sale time, not tune time.
  • Inspections & dealer visits — return to stock for peace of mind whenever you prefer. Worth knowing either way: the tune is emissions-intact and 5-gas verified regardless — this is about preference, not concealment.
  • Warranty conversations — a tune only becomes a factor if it's shown to cause a problem, which is exactly why ours is built fully reversible with emissions equipment untouched. The details live on the warranty & emissions page.
