Being fair to the gadget

Throttle controllers do the one thing they claim: make the pedal feel more responsive, cheaply, with a plug-in install you can move between vehicles. Reputable makers don't claim added power — and neither do we on their behalf. If pedal deadness is your only complaint, it's a legitimate budget option. The catch: most owners' complaints aren't just the pedal. Gear hunting, lazy shifts, drivability on larger tires, towing manners — those live in the calibration, and no pedal device can reach them.

Why we build response into the tune instead

  • It's the real thing, not a workaround — the calibration is how the truck runs, not a translator between your foot and the factory settings.
  • Toggleable throttle settings — response modes live in the tune; no extra box, no extra wiring on the pedal circuit.
  • The whole picture gets fixed at once — throttle, shift logic, and drivability tuned together, matched to your year, engine, and how you drive.
  • Emissions intact, 5-gas verified, reversible — and installed in person, so your questions get answered at the truck.
