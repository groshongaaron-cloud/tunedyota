## 1. Scope
2nd-gen Tundra (XK50, 2007–2021), all engines. Procedure verified on **2007 5.7L 4WD (3UR-FE)**; per-engine capacities pulled from each engine's own FSM. ⚠️ **Oil is engine- AND year-sensitive** — viscosity evolved 5W-20/5W-30 → 0W-20 across the generation; the video MUST show the per-engine/per-year table on-screen, not one blanket number.

## 2. FSM-Verified Specs (verbatim + FSM page)

**Per-engine oil capacity & viscosity (with filter change):**
| Engine | Viscosity (FSM) | Capacity w/ filter | Source manual · page |
|---|---|---|---|

| 4.7L V8 **2UZ-FE** (2007) | SAE **5W-30** (API SL / ILSAC) | **6.2 L (6.6 US qt)** | 4.7L-2UZ-FE 4WD 2007 · p.12066/12067 |
| 5.7L V8 **3UR-FE** (2007) | SAE **5W-20 or 0W-20** (API SL / ILSAC GF-4) | **7.0 L (7.4 US qt)** — 6.6 L w/o filter | 5.7L-3UR-FE 4WD 2007 · p.12225/12226 |
| 4.6L V8 **1UR-FE** (2010) | SAE **0W-20** (ILSAC) | **7.5 L (7.9 US qt)** | 4.6L-1UR-FE 4WD 2010 · p.12359/12360 |

**Torque & parts (5.7L ref, p.12224):**
- Oil pan **drain plug: 40 N·m (30 ft·lbf)** — install a **new gasket**
- Cartridge oil-**filter cap: 25 N·m (18 ft·lbf)** (SST 09228-06501); filter-housing drain plug 13 N·m (9 ft·lbf)
- Oil filter OEM ref **04152-YZZA4** (cartridge-type)

**Interval (5.7L maintenance schedule, p.2210–2216):** normal service listed at **5,000 mi / 6 mo** (severe) with longer normal-service intervals in the schedule. ⚠️ Interval also evolved (later 0W-20 MYs moved to 10,000 mi) — confirm per model year.

## 3. Procedure Outline (5.7L; cartridge filter)
Drain oil (pan plug, new gasket, 40 N·m) → remove cartridge filter cap with SST 09228-06501, replace element + O-rings → refill to spec capacity for the engine → run, check level, check for leaks. V6/4.7 use a different capacity (see table); confirm cartridge vs spin-on per engine on-screen.
