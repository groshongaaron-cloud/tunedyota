# Octane — Rocky's Sidekick — Character Brief

Date: 2026-08-05
Status: Canonical concept locked (owner-picked)

## Identity
**Octane** is Rocky's younger **apprentice** — a smaller, younger, high-energy fox
kit. Rounder/more youthful proportions than Rocky (bigger head and paws, perky
ears, big bright eager eyes). Same art family as Rocky: bold clean dark outline,
flat cel shading, stylized-3D cartoon-mascot look.

## Color of record
**Toyota Solar Octane Pearl — color code 4W5** (two-stage basecoat/clearcoat;
official touch-up part `00258-004W5-21`). Vibrant red-orange. **Color-match off a
real 4W5 swatch — do not eyeball a hex.** Off-white muzzle/chest/white tail-tip;
dark brown paws. This mirrors Rocky's Lunar Rock 6X3 discipline — the cast is named
and colored from the Toyota paint deck.

## Personality & role
Eager, enthusiastic, over-eager rookie — jumps ahead, over-torques, grabs the wrong
size socket. **Never dumb — just green and excited.** He's the **viewer surrogate**:
he asks the questions the audience is thinking so Rocky (the calm master tech) can
teach through banter. The two-hander drives engagement and doubles the short-form
skit material.

## Signature gag / prop
The **socket wrench** — Octane always has one, and the running bit is him grabbing
the **wrong-size socket** until Rocky hands him the right one. (Folds in the owner's
"Socket" name idea as Octane's signature tool.)

## Wardrobe (owner rules)
- Junior Tuned Yota gear: TY **hoodie or tee** + a **backwards** baseball cap.
- Wardrobe states: **cap** or **no cap**.
- **NO hood-up for Octane** (owner rule 2026-08-05) — the hood-up look is Rocky's.

## Cast / naming system
Characters are named and colored from the Toyota paint deck — expandable:
- **Rocky** ← Lunar Rock **6X3** (master tech).
- **Octane** ← Solar Octane **4W5** (apprentice).
- Future candidates: Cavalry Blue ("Cav"), Barcelona Red ("Barca"), etc.
