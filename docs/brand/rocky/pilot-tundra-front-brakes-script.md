## Part 3 — The Fix

*[Rocky narrating with on-screen exploded-view diagrams traced from reference frames. Each step beat is keyed to a frame. Rocky gestures toward diagrams; the diagram carries the "where," Rocky carries the "why."]*

### Step 1 — Wheel Off

*[On-screen: diagram of wheel assembly, lug nuts called out. [ref frame 003]]*

**VO:**

"Loosen the lug nuts before you jack the truck. Break them while the wheel is on the ground — don't strip them in the air."

"Jack the front corner at the correct jack point. Set a jack stand. Finish removing the lug nuts and pull the wheel."

*[Caption: lug nut loosening sequence. Pause beat.]*

### Step 2 — Caliper Removal

*[On-screen: exploded diagram of caliper, caliper bolts, caliper bracket. [ref frame 007]]*

**VO:**

"Two bolts hold the caliper to the bracket. `⟨VERIFY: caliper bolt size and torque spec⟩`. Remove them and slide the caliper off the rotor."

"Do not let the caliper hang by the brake line. Use a wire or bungee to support it from the spring or strut. One kinked line is a brake bleed job you did not plan for."

*[On-screen callout: caliper support hanging from coil spring. [ref frame 009]]*

### Step 3 — Caliper Bracket Removal

*[On-screen: caliper bracket bolts called out. [ref frame 011]]*

**VO:**

"The bracket is what the caliper slides on. Remove the two bracket bolts — `⟨VERIFY: caliper bracket bolt torque⟩` — and pull the bracket off the hub."

"Use a wire brush on the contact surfaces on the bracket where the pad ears sit. Clean metal means the new pads slide freely."

### Step 4 — Rotor Removal

*[On-screen: rotor with retaining screw called out if applicable. [ref frame 013]]*

**VO:**

"The rotor should slide straight off the hub. `⟨VERIFY: whether a retaining screw is present on this generation — remove it if so⟩`."

"If the rotor is seized to the hub, thread two bolts into the rotor's puller holes and tighten evenly to break it free. Do not hammer the rotor face."

### Step 5 — New Rotor Installation

*[On-screen: clean hub face, new rotor being seated. [ref frame 016]]*

**VO:**

"Wipe the hub face clean — any rust or debris here goes straight into rotor runout. Seat the new rotor. `⟨VERIFY: whether a retaining screw is specified for reinstallation⟩`."

"Hit the rotor face lightly with brake cleaner and let it flash off before the pads go on."

### Step 6 — Bracket Reinstall + New Pad Seating

*[On-screen: bracket torque sequence, pad contact points. [ref frame 019]]*

**VO:**

"Reinstall the bracket. Torque to `⟨VERIFY: caliper bracket torque spec in ft-lb⟩`. That is the spec — do not guess it."

"Apply a thin layer of slide pin grease to the pad contact rails on the bracket. Install the new pads — inner pad, outer pad. Confirm they seat fully in the bracket ears without binding."

### Step 7 — Caliper Piston Wind-Back

*[On-screen: caliper piston, wind-back tool. [ref frame 022]]*

**VO:**

"Before the caliper goes back on, the piston needs to retract to make room for the new, thicker pads."

"Pop the reservoir cap and watch the fluid level while you do this — fluid displaced from the caliper goes back to the reservoir. Have a rag ready. Use the wind-back tool or a C-clamp against the old pad backing plate to push the piston straight back in. Slow and even."

*[On-screen callout: reservoir cap location. Caption: "Watch fluid level while retracting."]*

### Step 8 — Caliper Reinstall

*[On-screen: caliper sliding over pads, caliper bolts torqued. [ref frame 026]]*

**VO:**

"Slide the caliper back over the new pads and rotor. Thread the caliper bolts in by hand first, then torque to `⟨VERIFY: caliper bolt torque spec in ft-lb⟩`."

"Confirm the pads are not pinched and the caliper slides freely on the pins."

### Step 9 — Wheel On + Lug Nuts

*[On-screen: wheel seated, lug nut torque sequence — star pattern. [ref frame 029]]*

**VO:**

"Mount the wheel and run the lug nuts in by hand. Lower the truck off the jack stand until the tire is on the ground and taking load. Torque the lug nuts in a star pattern to `⟨VERIFY: lug nut torque spec in ft-lb⟩`."

### Step 10 — Brake Pedal Bed-In

*[On-screen: diagram of pedal pumping sequence. Rocky reassure expression. [ref frame 031]]*

**VO:**

"Before you drive anywhere — pump the brake pedal. Slow strokes, full travel. You are pushing the piston back out and seating the pads against the rotor. Do this until the pedal feels firm and returns to height."

"If the pedal stays spongy after ten pumps, stop and check your work. A soft pedal means the pads are not seated or the piston did not retract fully."
