## Script (Rocky VO — original TY wording)
- **Hook:** "Toyota Tacoma oil change — no shop required. Here's the whole job."
- **1.** Get it up on ramps and drain the oil while it's warm.
- **2.** Pull the belly pan up front — it's just a few bolts.
- **3.** Reinstall the drain plug and snug it to spec. Don't over-tighten it.
- **4.** This Tacoma runs a cartridge filter. Drain the housing first, then drop in the new one.
- **5.** Belly pan goes right back on.
- **6.** Refill with full synthetic. We run **AMSOIL Signature Series** — fill to your capacity.
- **7.** Top off the washer fluid, back down to level ground, and check your oil.
- **Outro:** "That's a clean oil change. Grab the oil from Tuned Yota, or let us handle it. Until next time — keep it undeniable."
