## 2. Ideal customer profile

Upper-Midwest Toyota/Lexus truck/SUV owners (Tacoma, Tundra, 4Runner, Land Cruiser, Sequoia, FJ, Lexus GX/LX/RX). Reliability- and legality-conscious (not the delete crowd); overland/tow/daily-driver. Triggers: daily gear-hunting/throttle-lag frustration; recent mods (tires/lift/armor) hurting drivability; wanting usable low-end torque for towing.

**New ICP signal (2026-08-01):** The 2024+ turbo Tacoma/4Runner owner is now explicitly targeted — a buyer who wants the biggest power gains from the factory turbo and is comfortable paying $650–$950 for a calibration. This is a higher-spend, newer-truck buyer and likely a slightly younger, more mod-oriented segment than the legacy N/A crowd.

## 12. Three highest-leverage moves (current)

**Move 1 — AMSOIL PC one-tap enroll at the booking confirmation / certificate moment**
This is still the highest-ROI single change. The tuned customer at certificate delivery is at peak satisfaction and already has a financial relationship. A one-click "Enroll as AMSOIL Preferred Customer under Tuned Yota — save up to 25% for life" button on the `book.html` success state or in the confirmation email converts a one-time buyer into a recurring revenue relationship. No new traffic needed; pure LTV expansion.

**Move 2 — Financing on high-ticket offers ($950+ turbo cal, $1,599+ packs, $4,495+ superchargers)**
The price ceiling has risen to $8,395 and the new turbo cal tier starts at $650. A financing partner (e.g., Affirm, Snap Finance, or a Toyota-focused credit partner) reduces the single-session commitment barrier on packs and supercharger jobs. Even a banner ("Finance from $X/month") on the supercharger and pack pages would recover lost deals from sticker shock. This is now more urgent than at baseline given the raised ceiling.

**Move 3 — Referral incentive wired to the post-book success state + Midwest Tuning Group**
The `leadSuccess` success state already shows Facebook + Midwest Tuning Group links. Adding a "Refer a friend — you both get a free OTT Update" prompt here captures referrals at peak enthusiasm. The Midwest Tuning Group (FB group 501008078456222) is the natural distribution channel; a pinned "referral code" post format makes it trackable. Low code cost; community already exists.
