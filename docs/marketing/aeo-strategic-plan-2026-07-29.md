# AEO Strategic Plan — Path to 3/3 + Winning Citation on Every Tracked Prompt

**Written 2026-07-29** from the same-day audit (26 prompts, 3× majority protocol).
Goal per Aaron: on every prompt where TY is not 3/3 cited, become 3/3 AND the
top-cited source. Current: 6 prompts won, 20 underperforming.

## What today's data proves about how citations are won

1. **Question-shaped, spec-rich TY pages get cited within ~2 days of going live.** The July per-vehicle supercharger pages already carry prompts 3, 4, 13; the garage pages carry S4. This is the highest-confidence, fastest lever we have.
2. **Prompt intent determines the winning content type.** Pricing/fitment/local intents → structured vendor pages (TY's strength). "Worth it?"/comparison intents → YouTube + owner testimony (TY's absence). Spec/interval intents → forum-grade technical content (toyota-club, BITOG, trail4runner).
3. **A dealer page can beat everything but the brand itself** (yotaxpedition's PedalMonster page, 3/3 on B2). TY's Magnuson-store playbook transfers to Banks.
4. **Reddit's citation share is falling** (14/15 → 7/15 in the main set) while official-vendor and YouTube share rises. Owned pages + video now outrank forum-seeding as investments.

## The five programs

Every underperforming prompt maps to one or more of these.

### P1 — Question-shaped page blocks (fastest; proven ~2-day ingestion)
Add verbatim-spec FAQ/answer blocks to existing pages, each headlined with the tracked prompt's literal question. Spec-claim rules apply throughout (quote verbatim, split generations, attribute manufacturer figures).

### P2 — Comparison pages with dyno proof (counters YouTube/testimonial winners)
TY was baseline-cited as the *dyno source* on OTT-vs-KDMax before losing it. Honest head-to-head pages (specs verbatim for both sides, TY dyno charts, real customer outcomes via add-review/add-dyno-proof) are the only owned-content answer to "which is better?" prompts.

### P3 — YouTube (@tunedyota, campaign already approved)
YouTube won or co-won prompts 1, 8, B1, B3. The approved video pipeline (AvaNeural spec, OFF THE GRIND structure) should sequence its first videos against the tracked prompts, not generic topics: OTT vs KDMax, "is a supercharged Tundra reliable" (long-term customer trucks), PedalMonster on a Tacoma.

### P4 — Venue presence where forums still decide (targeted, not broad)
ih8mud owns the LX 570/LC200 VFTuner conversation (S3) — TY's tuners are licensed VFTuner PROs and belong in those exact threads. r/4Runner still carries 5, 9. Reddit-scout digests pick the threads; helpful-expert answers only.
### P5 — Local + dealer-network signals (no content writing required)
S6 is lost to a stale Yelp listing and two suburban dealer microsites; B5 to Banks' own locator. Fixes are listings, not pages: claim/create the AMSOIL-dealer local listings (Google Business Profile + Yelp, Lakeville MN), confirm TY appears in Banks' dealer locator during onboarding, and add Twin Cities language to /where-to-buy-amsoil.
