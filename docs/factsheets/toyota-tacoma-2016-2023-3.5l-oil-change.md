## Procedure (TY original wording, FSM-accurate)
1. Warm the engine ~1 min; park on level ground and secure it.
2. Remove the **No. 1 engine under cover** — **4 bolts (12 mm)**.
3. Drain the oil — remove the **oil-pan drain plug (14 mm)**; let it fully drain.
4. Cartridge filter: remove the **filter-cap drain plug** to drain the housing, then remove the **filter cap** and swap in the new element (+ new cap O-ring).
5. Reinstall the **filter-cap drain plug → 10 ft-lb (13 N·m)**; reinstall the **filter cap → 18 ft-lb (25 N·m)**.
6. Reinstall the **oil-pan drain plug with a NEW gasket → 30 ft-lb (40 N·m)**.
7. Reinstall the under cover — **4 bolts → 22 ft-lb (30 N·m)**.
8. Refill with **AMSOIL Signature Series 0W-20** — **6.1 qt (no oil cooler) / 6.2 qt (with oil cooler)**.
9. Run, check for leaks, then check the level on level ground and top off as needed.
